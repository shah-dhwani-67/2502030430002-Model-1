FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I see results from digital marketing?
SEO typically shows results in 3-6 months, while paid ads can generate leads from day one. Social media growth usually takes 2-4 months to build momentum. We set realistic timelines during our free consultation.
What is the minimum contract period?
We recommend a minimum of 3 months for meaningful results, but we don't lock you into long contracts. Our monthly plans are flexible, and most clients stay long-term because they see results.
Do you work with small businesses and startups?
Absolutely! We have packages starting from ₹15,000/month and work with businesses of all sizes — from solo founders to large enterprises. We tailor our strategy to your specific budget and goals.
How do you report on campaign performance?
We provide weekly or monthly reports depending on your plan, covering key metrics like traffic, leads, conversions, ROAS, and revenue impact. Growth plan clients get access to a real-time dashboard.
Will I have a dedicated account manager?
Growth and Enterprise plan clients get a dedicated account manager as their single point of contact. Starter plan clients have access to our team via email and scheduled calls.
